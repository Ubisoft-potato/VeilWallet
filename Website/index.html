02 / Privacy model

Keys stay on your Mac.

Import a 12- or 24-word recovery phrase or a supported private key into a password-protected vault. Signing happens locally. Use watch-only mode to follow public addresses without importing keys.

Read the security model ↗

Tor starts after unlock. Veil passes hostnames through its Tor proxy and blocks network actions whenever Tor is disconnected.

Install Veil Wallet.

Available as a DMG for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.

Download DMG ↓ All releases ↗

On your Mac

  1. Download the DMG and matching checksums.
  2. Verify the download, open the DMG, and drag Veil Wallet into Applications.
  3. Launch Veil Wallet and create or unlock your local vault.
